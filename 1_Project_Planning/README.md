# Project Planning

## Project
Goldman Sachs Financial Performance & Business Segment Analysis

## Business Problem
Goldman Sachs operates multiple financial businesses whose performance can vary across market conditions and economic environments. Management needs a clear view of historical financial performance, profitability, operating expenses, and business segment results to identify areas of strength, weakness, and potential future opportunity.

## Objective
Analyze Goldman Sachs’ historical financial performance using publicly available financial data to evaluate revenue, profitability, expenses, business segment performance, and key financial metrics. Develop an executive financial dashboard and provide data-driven observations about the company’s financial trends.

## Analysis Scope

### Firmwide Financial Analysis
2021–2025

### Business Segment Analysis
2023–2025

The shorter segment-analysis period is used because Goldman Sachs reorganized its reporting structure in late 2022 into Global Banking & Markets, Asset & Wealth Management, and Platform Solutions, making the post-reorganization years more directly comparable.

## Business Questions
1. How have Goldman Sachs’ net revenues and net earnings changed over time?
2. How have operating expenses changed relative to revenue?
3. Which business segments contribute the most revenue, and how has that changed over time?
4. How has Goldman Sachs’ profitability changed across the analysis period?
5. What do key financial metrics such as ROE, EPS, and profit margin reveal about performance?
6. Based on historical trends, what financial patterns should management or investors monitor going forward?

## Data Sources
The project will use publicly available Goldman Sachs financial information, including annual reports, earnings releases, and regulatory filings.

## Project Approach
1. Collect and organize historical Goldman Sachs financial data.
2. Build a structured financial dataset in Google Sheets.
3. Calculate and interpret key financial metrics.
4. Validate and prepare the data for analysis.
5. Analyze financial trends using SQL and BigQuery.
6. Use Python for additional financial analysis and forecasting where appropriate.
7. Build an executive financial dashboard in Tableau.
8. Summarize findings and recommendations in a final stakeholder report.

## Data Transparency
All historical company financial figures used in this project will come from publicly available sources. Any forecasts, estimates, or analyst-created calculations will be clearly identified and will not be presented as Goldman Sachs internal forecasts or confidential company data.
